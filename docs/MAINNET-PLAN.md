## Stop conditions

Any of these stops the plan; nothing further is sent, the evidence (including `-INCOMPLETE` files and the spend ledgers in
`evidence/spend/`) is kept, and resuming needs a new typed go:

1. A preflight is NOT READY: balance below the step's worst case, a pending nonce, code at a predicted address, a max fee above 40 gwei, an existing `fixtures/deployments-mainnet.json`.
2. The spend guard refuses a transaction (per-transaction cap, step total, fee ceiling); a live gas estimate above 1.5 × its planned bound.
3. Any receipt reverts; a funded key does not receive exactly the planned value.
4. Post-cutover verification fails (network, release, order signer, deployer key on the server, gate) — the mainnet service is stopped.
5. The gate is not closed before acceptance or the lifecycle (`publicExecution` true or any probe other than 503).
6. A run with an unexpected decision or outcome, an unverified fee or business transaction, a passport with a FAIL finding, or measured outflow that would pass a cap.
7. A business transaction left `BROADCAST_ATTEMPTED` for more than 10 minutes: reconcile first, no new run.
8. The RPC reports a chain other than 5042, or the running release is not `964a61b` (or an approved successor).
9. A journaled deployment step whose nonce was consumed by a transaction the journal does not know, a reverted step, or a deployer nonce that moved outside the journal: the deploy stops for a manual check and nothing is re-signed (a re-run reconciles and continues only when the chain agrees with the journal).
10. The plan ledger refuses: a step whose committed total across every invocation plus the next worst case would pass its cap, a plan total that would pass the 0.69 USDC budget cap, or an environment override above a pinned cap or above the pinned 40 gwei fee ceiling — each before anything is signed or started. Mainnet never runs without a named plan (a blank `PLAN_ID` is refused).
11. The running release does not serve the manifest-based evidence route (audit API-01; served since `40c5858`, live since 2026-10-10 07:27Z), or the operator has not decided on the server keys' handling after the evidence-route incident (see the approvals below).

## Resumability and the plan ledger (review of 2026-10-10)

- **Deployment journal** `evidence/deploy-mainnet-journal.json`: written before every broadcast (nonce, hash, the identical signed bytes) and after every receipt. A restart of `deploy` reconciles each signed step from the chain — confirmed if mined, waited for if the node holds it, the identical bytes handed to the node again if it does not and the nonce is still free — and continues with the unfinished steps only; a confirmed step is never deployed again. The manifest `fixtures/deployments-mainnet.json` is written from the journal once every step is confirmed. Rehearsed on the testnet with two interruptions (after a confirmation, after a broadcast): `evidence/selftest/deploy-resume-rehearsal-2026-10-10/`.
- **Plan record** `evidence/spend/mainnet-plan-gate-d-mainnet-2026-10.json` (plan id `gate-d-mainnet-2026-10`): every spending step writes its settled outflow, pending commitments and — for acceptance and the lifecycle — the balance baseline of its first invocation, so a restart measures from where the step began. The approved caps above are pinned in the record; `SPEND_CAP_USDC`, `LIFECYCLE_PROJECT_CAP_USDC`, `DEPLOY_*`, `FUND_*`, `PROBE_*` may lower a cap for a run, never raise it. The per-step spend ledgers `evidence/spend/mainnet-<step>.json` stay the transaction-level record. `ops/mainnet-cutover.sh preflight` prints the record (`scripts/lib/plan-status.ts`); commit the record with each step's evidence (step 4 and step 10) so the accounting is kept with the history.
- **Lifecycle restart** (found in the `40c5858` rehearsal on 2026-10-10: the script died on a dropped connection right after it had started the interruption scenario, the server finished the run on its own, and the plan record kept the scenario open at its worst case with no way to settle it — a re-run was refused by the cap): every open scenario now carries its durable identity in the record — the interruption scenario its admission key, written before the POST; the wallet scenario its order, attempt and hash as it learns them — and a re-run of `ops/mainnet-cutover.sh lifecycle` settles them FIRST: the same admission key re-sent returns the original run, which is followed to its outcome and verified and then counts as complete under the plan; a wallet hash is awaited; the read-only preflight shows the same decisions. A record without identities stops the step until the operator settles it explicitly (`LIFECYCLE_SETTLE_OPEN=<open id>`) after checking the server's state. Rehearsed on the testnet against the public instance: `evidence/selftest/lifecycle-resume-rehearsal-2026-10-10/` (ACCEPTANCE AP33-104, EVIDENCE 2026-10-10 rows).
- **Acceptance restart** (review of `bf0c800`, P1): the acceptance script used to budget a complete new batch before recovering anything, so a pending commitment could make its own recovery unreachable, and its recovery re-sent `APPROVED_PAYMENT` for every reserved key and read a lost answer or a 503 as "nothing started". Now every run is recorded under `run-<round>-<fixture>:key:<admission key>` before the admission request leaves (and `:run:<id>` once named); a re-run of `ops/mainnet-cutover.sh acceptance` recovers the open operations first with their own fixture and key, keeps the reservation on any answer that proves nothing (lost answer, `503 EXECUTION_GATED`, `409`) and stops, releases it only on a refusal that proves the key was never bound, re-verifies the positions completed earlier from the server's record, and budgets only the remaining positions — in the dry run as in the execution. Review of `0d1088e`: a released reservation is recorded apart from settled operations and is unfinished work, PASS needs exactly one verified report per planned position (anything less is INCOMPLETE), and the runner/creator balances must cover the remaining work only — re-checked after recovery before anything new starts. Tested end to end against a fake API and RPC (`scripts/test/mainnet-recovery.test.ts`; ACCEPTANCE AP33-109/110, AP33-119, AP33-122).
